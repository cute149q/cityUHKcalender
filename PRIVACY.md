# Privacy Policy

CityU Calendar Exporter reads course names, meeting times, dates, locations, and instructors from the AIMS **Student Detail Schedule** page that you have open. This information is used only to create an iCalendar (`.ics`) file on your device.

The extension does not collect, transmit, store on a server, sell, or share timetable data, AIMS credentials, cookies, browsing history, or personal information. Your optional language preference is stored locally in your browser.
